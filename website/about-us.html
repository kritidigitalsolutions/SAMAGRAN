About Us

Inspired by Lal Bhandar's Legacy Since 1961

From booking sacred rituals to ordering authentic Puja essentials, Samagran makes every spiritual experience simple, respectful, and seamless.

Since 1961, Lal Bhandar has been serving its community as a trusted family-run grocery store, offering everyday essentials along with basic puja samagri that have been a part of countless homes, celebrations, and sacred rituals for generations.

Over the years, one thing became evident - while devotion remains timeless, the spiritual needs of devotees continue to evolve. Finding authentic puja essentials, booking religious services, and accessing spiritual offerings often remain fragmented and inconvenient.

Inspired by this understanding and built upon decades of trust, Samagran was born.

Samagran is our vision to go beyond traditional offerings and create a modern spiritual ecosystem that brings together everything a devotee may need in one place. From authentic puja samagri and customized puja kits to sacred rituals and spiritual services, we are committed to making spirituality more accessible, authentic, and convenient.

While our journey begins in a small town, our aspiration is to serve devotees across India by honouring traditions and embracing innovation. We believe that spirituality should never be limited by geography - it should be available to every devotee, wherever they are.

At Samagran, we are not just building a platform; we are carrying forward a legacy of trust and reimagining it for the spiritual India of tomorrow.
